# Electron Wave-Particle Duality Explorer

An interactive educational application demonstrating the fundamental concepts of quantum physics, specifically the wave-particle duality of electrons.

## Overview

This project explores the dual nature of electrons, showing how they can behave both as particles and waves depending on the experimental conditions. The application includes interactive simulations of key quantum physics experiments that demonstrate these phenomena.

## Features

- Interactive experiments demonstrating wave-particle duality
- Photoelectric effect simulation
- Electron diffraction demonstration
- Double-slit experiment with observer effect
- Quantum tunneling in batteries
- Semiconductor transistor operation
- Quantum maze solving simulation
- Electron microscopy comparison
- 3D atomic models
- Models of Sommerfeld and Schrodinger atoms with external links

## Key Experiments

### Photoelectric Effect
Shows how light behaves as particles (photons) when ejecting electrons from a metal surface.

### Electron Diffraction
Demonstrates that electron beams produce diffraction patterns like waves when passing through a crystal.

### Double Slit Experiment
Shows how electrons create interference patterns when behaving as waves, but act as particles when observed.

### Quantum Tunneling
Illustrates how electrons can "tunnel" through energy barriers that they classically shouldn't be able to pass.

### Semiconductor Transistors
Shows how quantum behavior in semiconductors enables precise control of electron flow.
